# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js and TypeScript for the workshop interface; Python 3.12, FastAPI, and Pydantic for the service; SQLite for local use and PostgreSQL for deployed operation. The credential-free experience replays deterministic fixtures. An optional OpenAI-compatible provider path adds schema-bound tool calls, evidence validation, retries, fallback, and cost and latency traces.

## Users

Operations employees, managers, transformation leads, and IT or security partners who need to understand repetitive work before changing it.

## Product Purpose

TaskBridge AI helps a team describe an everyday workflow, map how it works now, decide whether rules, AI assistance, a hybrid approach, or no change is appropriate, simulate a controlled pilot, explain the proposal to each audience, measure synthetic results, and prepare a handoff package.

Success means a team can understand the recommendation, trace it to captured workflow evidence, see the human control points, and reproduce the demonstration locally without credentials.

## Positioning

TaskBridge does not begin with a model or assume AI is the answer. It begins with the work, compares four intervention choices, and makes the reasoning legible to the people who perform, manage, approve, and secure the process.

## Operating Context

The first release includes synthetic shared-inbox triage, weekly status reporting, and invoice-exception review. The workshop captures triggers, systems, steps, decisions, exceptions, frequency, time, error cost, data sensitivity, and reversibility. All consequential actions remain simulated.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Guided workflow interview and current-state map.
- Explainable rules, AI-assist, hybrid, or no-change recommendation.
- Deterministic pilot simulator with evidence, confidence, exceptions, and human checkpoints.
- Optional model-backed analysis with versioned prompts, structured tool calls, and inspectable traces.
- Role-gated API access, PostgreSQL persistence, and failure-safe deterministic fallback.
- Role-specific explanations for employees, managers, executives, and IT or security.
- Exportable pilot charter, SOP, measures, risks, rollback plan, and training guide.
- Synthetic email, spreadsheet, and ticket-queue adapters only in v0.2.0.
- No real company integrations, external writes, or model credentials are required.
- Unknown facts remain unknown; outcome figures are labeled synthetic and never presented as company results.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is TaskBridge AI. Its voice is plain, practical, respectful of employee expertise, and comfortable saying that AI is not the right answer. Public product language must not mention recruiting, employment applications, portfolios, or showcasing skills.

## Evidence on Hand

Only synthetic scenarios, fixture traces, and deterministic evaluation results are available. There are no customer deployments, testimonials, production outcomes, or commercial benchmarks, and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Listen to the work before choosing the technology.
2. Explain every recommendation in ordinary language.
3. Keep people in control of consequential decisions.
4. Measure the workflow, not merely the model.
5. Leave behind an operable process and honest limitations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web interface must meet WCAG AA contrast, support complete keyboard operation, respect reduced motion, and avoid assuming prior AI knowledge.
