# Quality bar

The first viewport must feel like a real process-improvement workshop rather than a generic AI dashboard. The current and proposed workflows carry the composition, intervention modes are understandable without a legend hunt, and the human decision point remains visually stronger than the model suggestion.

The interface must preserve the physical clarity of binder tabs, movable process tiles, review marks, and an ordered facilitation path without becoming decorative stationery. Every surface must answer three questions quickly: what work is being examined, why this intervention was chosen, and where people retain authority.

The mobile layout may use horizontal lanes for the process itself, but the document must never create page-level horizontal overflow or hide the workshop stage controls.
